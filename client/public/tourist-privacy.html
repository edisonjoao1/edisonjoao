Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

The short version: Tourist uses location, microphone, and trip context only to provide the guide and interpreter features you choose. We do not sell personal data, show third-party ads, or track you across other companies' apps.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

Tourist stores your onboarding choices, interests, guide style, narration depth, saved places, visited places, tour history, walking paths, and subscription entitlement locally on your device. This information supports your passport, preferences, and trip history.

2. Location

When you start a Walk Here route, Tourist uses your location to find nearby points of interest, guide the route, trigger relevant stories, and record the path for your local trip history. Location can continue while an active narrated walk is in the background if you grant the required permission. Tourist does not send precise coordinates to Firebase Analytics.

3. Voice, text, and travel context

When you request narration or use the live interpreter, Tourist sends the content needed for that feature directly from your device to OpenAI. Depending on the feature, this can include microphone audio, speech transcripts, translated text, the selected language, nearby place information, city context, interests, guide style, narration depth, and recent conversation context.

Live microphone audio is processed for the active interpreter session and is not added to your Tourist passport. Generated narration and translation output may be held in memory or temporary app storage as needed for playback.

4. Maps, search, and place images

Tourist uses Apple MapKit and local search for maps, routes, addresses, and nearby places. Place images can be requested from Wikimedia Commons or Wikipedia. Those providers can receive standard network information such as your IP address when a request is made.

5. Product analytics

Release builds use Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature adoption and improve the product. Analytics can receive an app-instance or device identifier, general location derived from a masked IP address, app and device information, app lifecycle events, and a limited set of product interactions such as onboarding completion, tab selection, a tour starting or completing, interpreter use, and paywall display. Firebase can also measure StoreKit purchase and subscription events with product ID, product name, and price.

Tourist does not send names, precise coordinates, walking paths, place names, free-form text, narration, microphone audio, speech transcripts, translations, or full payment details to Firebase Analytics. Debug builds disable Analytics collection.

6. Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and payment information. Tourist receives only the product and entitlement information needed to unlock Tourist Plus. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

7. Service providers

  • OpenAI: narration, speech, and live translation processing
  • Google Firebase: sanitized product analytics in release builds
  • Apple: maps, local search, location, app distribution, and subscriptions
  • Wikimedia Foundation services: publicly available place images and metadata

8. Advertising, tracking, and sale of data

Tourist does not display third-party advertising, sell personal data, or use your activity for cross-app tracking. Analytics is used to understand Tourist itself, not to build advertising profiles.

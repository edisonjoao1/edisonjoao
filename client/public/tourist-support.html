Support

Simple help for getting back to your trip.

Contact

We normally respond within 1 or 2 business days. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Send a message

Do not send passwords, private recordings, or payment information.

Walk Here

Tourist cannot find my location.

Open iPhone Settings, find Tourist, and allow location access. Return to Tourist in an open area and try Walk Here again.

Narration stopped in the background.

Confirm an active walk is still open and that media volume is audible. Reopen Tourist and resume the walk if iOS paused playback.

Interpreter

The interpreter cannot hear me.

Open iPhone Settings, find Tourist, and enable Microphone. Move closer to the phone, speak a complete phrase, and try again with a stable internet connection.

The translation session will not start.

Stop any active narrated walk, confirm your internet connection, then reopen the Translate tab and tap Start Interpreter.

Tourist Plus

I subscribed, but Plus is not active.

Open Profile, choose Tourist Plus, and tap Restore Purchases. Confirm you are using the same Apple Account that completed the purchase.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel Tourist Plus in your App Store subscriptions. Apple handles renewal, cancellation, billing, and refund requests.

Privacy

Read the Tourist Privacy Policy for details about location, microphone use, local trip data, live processing, and analytics.

Introduction

This explains how Accountability Buddy, AI Amigo, Sober AI, Inteligencia Artificial, Activi Match, and future apps by Edison João handle information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. We only access anonymous app store data (like download numbers).

Third-Party AI Services

Some of our apps use third-party AI services (such as OpenAI) to power features like chat, analysis, and suggestions. When you use these features:

  • Your messages and relevant app data are sent to the AI provider to generate a response.
  • We do not store this data on any server — it is sent directly from your device to the AI provider.
  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone) are included in these requests.
  • The AI provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle the data they receive.

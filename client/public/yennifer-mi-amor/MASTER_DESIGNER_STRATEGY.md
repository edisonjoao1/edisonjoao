## Vision: Museum-Quality Digital Experience

Transform from "website" to **immersive brand experience** that rivals Agent Provocateur, Coco de Mer, and La Perla.

## Photography Strategy

### Image Sources (Free High-Quality Stock)
1. **Unsplash** - Professional editorial photography
2. **Pexels** - Curated lingerie/fashion shots
3. **Pixabay** - Additional options

### Search Terms
- "lingerie editorial"
- "intimate apparel"
- "boudoir photography"
- "lace lingerie"
- "black lingerie"
- "luxury lingerie"

### Image Specifications
- **Resolution**: Minimum 2000px width
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait for products
- **Style**: Editorial, high-contrast, professional lighting
- **Composition**: Negative space, artistic framing
- **Color**: Black, burgundy, nude tones priority

## Master-Level Enhancements

### 1. Cinematic Product Reveals
- Ken Burns effect (slow zoom + pan)
- Crossfade transitions between hover states
- Parallax layers (foreground/background separation)

### 2. Gallery Modal System
- Full-screen lightbox
- Swipe/keyboard navigation
- Zoom on click (2x, 4x)
- Thumbnail navigation strip

### 3. Immersive Backgrounds
- High-res lifestyle photos (blurred backdrop)
- Video backgrounds (silk, fabric textures)
- Cinemagraphs (subtle movement)

### 4. Editorial Lookbook Section
- Full-width hero images
- Text overlays with story
- "Shop the Look" hotspots
- Scroll-triggered reveals

### 5. Sophisticated Hover States
- Image swap (front/back views)
- Color fade effect
- Zoom region focus
- Product detail callouts

### 6. Brand Film Integration
- Hero video background
- Muted, looping, 4K quality
- Slow-motion fabric movement
- Professional color grading

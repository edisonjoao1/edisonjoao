Information you save

Drafts, preferences, and other content may be stored on your device. Apps with accounts or synced features may also store information online so you can continue using those features. Uninstalling an app removes its local data, but does not necessarily remove online data.

Connected features

When you choose to use writing, chat, image, voice, analysis, or other connected features, the app may send your request, relevant context, and any text or media you select to external providers, sometimes through our service, to produce the result. Some apps retain conversations or generated content so you can return to them. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and purchases

Some apps use Firebase for account verification, analytics, crash diagnostics, or notifications. These services may process device identifiers and usage information. Apple handles in-app purchases and subscriptions; we do not receive your payment card details.

Your choices

You can remove local data by deleting the app. For questions about information stored online, or to request access or deletion, contact us. If your app uses Plaid, read its separate security and privacy policy.

Need help?

Make sure you have the latest app version and try reopening it. If something still is not working, tell us the app name and what happened.

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to all apps developed by Edison João.

Data We Collect

▼

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. All your data stays on your device. We only have access to anonymous App Store metrics like download counts.

Third-Party AI Services

▼

Some of our apps use third-party AI services to power features like chat, analysis, and suggestions. When you use these features:

  • Your messages and relevant app data are sent to the AI provider to generate a response.
  • We do not store this data on any server — it is sent directly from your device to the AI provider.
  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone) are included in these requests.
  • The AI provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle the data they receive.

Apps that use AI features will ask for your consent before any data is sent. Select an app above to see exactly what data it sends.

Analytics

▼

Some apps use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (screen views, feature usage, crash reports). This data contains no personal information and is used solely to improve app quality.

Data Security

▼

Your data stays on your device. Since we don't collect or store personal data on any server, there is no centralized database that could be compromised. All communication with third-party services uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Your Rights

▼

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app. For AI-powered features, you can revoke consent within the app's settings.

Changes to This Policy

▼

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.

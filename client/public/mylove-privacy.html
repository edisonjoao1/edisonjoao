Days Together: Love Tracker - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

Days Together: Love Tracker ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

Key Point: Your personal data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or have access to your relationship details, photos, or personal information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT collect:

  • Your name or your partner's name
  • Your relationship start date or anniversary
  • Photos or images you add to the App
  • Moments, memories, or personal notes
  • AI-generated content (love notes, poems, etc.)
  • Your location
  • Your contacts

All of this information is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Information We May Collect

We collect limited, anonymous information to improve the App:

  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics (e.g., which features are most popular, app crashes) through Firebase Analytics. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Purchase Information: Apple handles all payment processing. We receive confirmation of purchases but do not have access to your payment details.

4. AI Content Generation

When you use AI features to generate love notes, date ideas, or other content:

  • Your prompts are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate responses
  • We do not store these prompts or responses on our servers
  • OpenAI's data handling is governed by their privacy policy
  • Generated content is stored only on your device

5. Data Storage

All your personal data (names, dates, photos, moments, saved content) is stored:

  • Locally on your device
  • In your iCloud account (if you have iCloud backup enabled)

We do not have access to this data. If you delete the App, your local data will be deleted. iCloud backups are managed by Apple.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): For anonymous usage analytics
  • OpenAI: For AI-powered content generation
  • Apple StoreKit: For in-app purchases and subscriptions

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

Since your personal data is stored on your device, you have full control over it:

  • Access: View all your data directly in the App
  • Delete: Delete individual items or uninstall the App to remove all data
  • Export: Share or export your content using the App's sharing features

9. Security

Your data is protected by:

  • iOS device encryption
  • Secure HTTPS connections for any network requests
  • Apple's App Store security requirements

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or App Store update notes. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store or visit our website.

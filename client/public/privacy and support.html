Troubleshooting

If you're experiencing issues, please follow these steps:

  • Ensure you have the latest version of the app installed.
  • Restart the app.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Clear Cache).
  • If problems persist, contact our support team.
